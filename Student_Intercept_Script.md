# Student Intercept Script (5-Minute Version)

**Goal:** Quickly validate the core problem and get permission for a longer follow-up interview.

**Context:** Use this when you have less than 5 minutes with a student, for example, outside a coaching center.

---

### **1. The Opener (30 seconds)**

- "Hey, quick question - you look like a serious student. My name is [Your Name]. I'm not a teacher, I'm developing an app to make studying less stressful."
- "I have one question for you, it'll take 30 seconds. Is that cool?"

### **2. The Killer Question (1-2 minutes)**

- "Have you ever had a week where you studied like crazy, but your test scores didn't move? How did that feel?"
- (Listen for: "Yes!", "All the time", "It's so frustrating", "I feel like I'm wasting my time"). This is your validation signal.
- (Follow up): "Right? It feels like you're doing something wrong but you don't know what."

### **3. The Hook & The Ask (1-2 minutes)**

- "I'm building an app that's like a private coach on your phone. It helps you figure out _how_ to study based on your energy, so you get better results for your effort. It's totally private."
- "You seem to really get this problem. I'm doing longer 20-minute interviews to get feedback from smart students like you. I'm offering a [₹250 Amazon Gift Card / Pizza Coupon] for your time."
- "Would you be open to a quick chat later this week? I can grab your WhatsApp number."

### **4. Wrap-up (30 seconds)**

- "Awesome, thanks so much. This is super helpful. I'll ping you to set up a time. Good luck with your studies!"
