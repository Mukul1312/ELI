# Content Architecture

The Eli product should use a small number of content roles repeatedly and deliberately.

Content should not behave like filler, motivation wallpaper, or analytical narration. It should help the student move through the product loop with less friction and more clarity.

## Core Message Types

### 1. Orientation

Orientation content answers:

- where the student is
- what matters right now
- what this moment is for

Orientation is especially important on arrival, transition, and closure surfaces.

## 2. Invitation

Invitation content makes the next action feel possible.

Its job is to:

- lower the cost of beginning
- make the action feel bounded
- reduce ambiguity without becoming commanding

Invitation is the primary content posture of commitment moments.

## 3. Confirmation

Confirmation content tells the student that something real counted.

Its job is to:

- make progress believable
- mark meaningful completion
- reinforce continuity without turning completion into hype

Confirmation should be calm and specific, not generic praise.

## 4. Reflection

Reflection content turns repeated evidence into meaning.

Its job is to:

- recognize a real pattern
- deepen continuity
- help the student see something useful without sounding presumptuous

Reflection should remain sparse.

## Content Distribution Across The Loop

| Loop stage | Primary content role |
|---|---|
| Return | Orientation |
| Orient | Orientation |
| Commit | Invitation |
| Work | Minimal support |
| Close | Confirmation |
| Mirror | Reflection |

## Content Across Product Objects

Content should respect the difference between `session`, `study thread`, and `curriculum context`.

- **Session language** should drive immediate action. It should help the student start, continue, or close the current bounded study moment.
- **Study thread language** should drive continuity. It should help the student recognize what line of work is still alive, resumable, or worth returning to.
- **Curriculum context language** should appear only when it clarifies what the thread belongs to. It must never sound like taxonomy management, syllabus administration, or academic bookkeeping.

## Conversational Content Roles

If conversational Eli exists, its content should stay within these allowed roles:

- orientation recall
- invitation support
- grounded confirmation context
- earned reflection

Conversational Eli should not become:

- analytics narration
- comparative summary
- planning-heavy instruction
- productivity-score language

## Content Behavior By State

The same content role should behave differently depending on current capacity.

### Gentle
- **Orientation:** should simplify and narrow attention quickly
- **Invitation:** should feel reversible, gentle, and low-ceremony
- **Confirmation:** should stay quiet and stabilizing rather than energizing
- **Reflection:** should be rare and extremely bounded

### Focus
- **Orientation:** should be practical and clarifying
- **Invitation:** should feel clear and manageable
- **Confirmation:** should reinforce that real progress counted
- **Reflection:** may appear lightly when it adds credible meaning

### Momentum
- **Orientation:** may be shorter and more direct
- **Invitation:** may be firmer and more forward-moving without becoming commanding
- **Confirmation:** may carry slightly stronger continuity into what comes next
- **Reflection:** may be more visible, but must still remain earned

## Content Behavior By Phase

Phase determines what the content is allowed to claim or imply.

### Arrival
- content should rely on immediate context
- orientation should not imply deep familiarity
- invitation should avoid "what usually works for you" framing
- reflection should usually be absent

### Attunement
- content may use light continuity and low-risk pattern language
- invitation may adapt more visibly to current conditions
- confirmation may carry more contextual continuity
- reflection may appear if it remains clearly evidence-bound

### Familiarity
- content may use more specific continuity
- invitation may sound more exact because Eli has earned stronger confidence
- confirmation may connect progress more clearly to recurring behavior
- reflection may be more precise, but must never become diagnostic or overly intimate

## Reflection: Appropriate Vs Premature

Reflection is appropriate when:

- the product has repeated evidence
- the language stays bounded to what Eli can support
- the reflection helps the student understand something useful

Reflection is premature when:

- the language sounds emotionally ahead of the evidence
- the product is still in `Arrival`
- the student is clearly in a strained `Gentle` moment
- the reflection feels like brand performance rather than recognition

## Content Rule

Every important piece of product copy should be doing one of these jobs:

- orient
- invite
- confirm
- reflect

If content does none of these, it is probably noise.

## What To Avoid

Avoid content that behaves like:

- generic praise
- motivational performance
- dashboard narration
- guilt-heavy recap
- decorative brand voice

The content system should remain useful, exact, and emotionally disciplined.
