# Phase Architecture

The Eli product should also adapt through relationship phases: `Arrival`, `Attunement`, and `Familiarity`.

Phases should not radically change the layout of the product. They should regulate what Eli is allowed to assume, reference, and reflect back to the student over time.

## What Phase Changes

At the product level, phase should change:

- how specific Eli can be
- how much continuity the product can express
- how much pattern language Eli can responsibly use
- how confidently `Mirror` can appear

Phase should **not** change:

- the core experience spine
- the ethical posture of the product
- the student's current-capacity state

## Arrival

`Arrival` is the phase where trust is still being established.

### Product behavior in Arrival
- Eli should rely on immediate context rather than history-heavy assumptions
- continuity should be light and non-presumptuous
- reflective language should be minimal or absent
- the experience should feel welcoming, clear, and safe
- the product should avoid acting like it "knows" the student already

### What Arrival protects against
- premature familiarity
- false pattern claims
- trust erosion caused by overreach

## Attunement

`Attunement` begins when Eli has enough evidence to adapt more visibly and responsibly.

### Product behavior in Attunement
- the product may begin carrying more continuity between visits
- Eli may use limited, low-risk observations about pacing or current load
- adaptation across density, warmth, and action energy may become more noticeable
- light reflective moments may appear if they remain clearly evidence-bound

### What Attunement protects against
- generic repetition
- underuse of trustworthy context
- adaptation that remains too weak to feel helpful

## Familiarity

`Familiarity` begins only when Eli has earned the right to be more specific and more continuous without becoming presumptuous.

### Product behavior in Familiarity
- Eli may refer to repeated patterns it can responsibly stand behind
- continuity between sessions may feel stronger and more useful
- `Mirror` may appear more credibly
- the product may preserve more meaningful carryover from previous work
- reflection may become more precise, but must remain bounded

### What Familiarity protects against
- flat genericity
- false neutrality when real evidence exists
- reflection that is too weak to feel meaningful

## Phase Variables

| Variable | Arrival | Attunement | Familiarity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specificity | Low | Moderate | High but evidence-bound |
| Continuity | Light | Noticeable | Strong |
| Pattern language | Avoided | Careful | Responsible |
| Reflection depth | Minimal | Limited | More credible |
| Relational confidence | Low | Growing | Earned |

## Product Rule

Phase should never be used to make Eli feel artificially intimate.

If the product sounds emotionally ahead of the evidence, the phase system has failed.
