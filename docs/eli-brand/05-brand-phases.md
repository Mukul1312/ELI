# Brand Phases

Eli's brand expression should mature with the relationship. The brand must not act like a stranger forever, but it must also not claim familiarity before it has earned it.

These phases are not defined by hard-coded session counts alone. They are primarily unlocked by **relationship evidence**: repeated return behavior, interaction diversity, signal confidence, and the degree to which Eli can make a specific observation without overreaching.

This document is only about **relationship maturity**. It does not define current-capacity states. Those belong in the brand-state model.

## Phase 1: Arrival

`Arrival` is the phase where trust is still being established.

- **What Eli knows:** Immediate, situational context only.
- **What Eli is allowed to assume:** Very little beyond what the student has directly given or what is obvious in the moment.
- **What Eli is allowed to adapt:** Basic tone, pacing, and simplicity. Eli may soften or clarify the experience, but should not imply deep knowledge.
- **What Eli must not claim:** Patterns, preferences, identity, or "what usually works" language.

### Brand posture in Arrival
- Calm and clear
- Welcoming without acting familiar
- Structured without over-explaining
- Easy to come back to

## Phase 2: Attunement

`Attunement` begins once Eli has enough evidence to adapt more visibly and responsibly.

- **What Eli knows:** Repeated behavior in enough moments to notice light patterns or recurring conditions.
- **What Eli is allowed to assume:** Limited, low-risk observations about fit, pacing, or current load.
- **What Eli is allowed to adapt:** More meaningful shifts in density, warmth, action energy, and voice posture.
- **What Eli must not claim:** Identity-level reflection or emotionally intimate certainty.

### Brand posture in Attunement
- More context-aware
- More fitting to the student's current condition
- More capable of fitting the experience to the student's current needs
- Still careful not to overstate what Eli knows

## Phase 3: Familiarity

`Familiarity` begins when Eli has earned the right to sound more specific and more personal without becoming presumptuous.

- **What Eli knows:** Patterns that are repeated enough to stand behind responsibly.
- **What Eli is allowed to assume:** Meaningful but bounded interpretation of rhythm, preference, and recurring behavior.
- **What Eli is allowed to adapt:** Reflective language, stronger continuity, and the `Mirror` layer.
- **What Eli must not claim:** Inner motives, psychological diagnosis, or false certainty about why the student behaves as they do.

### Brand posture in Familiarity
- More precise
- More continuous from one interaction to the next
- More capable of turning observation into meaning
- Still grounded in evidence rather than intimacy theater
