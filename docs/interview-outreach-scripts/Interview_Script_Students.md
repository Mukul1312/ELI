# Interview Script: Students (JEE/NEET Aspirants)

## Goal of this Interview

To validate the emotional experience of "Silent Decay" and to test the desirability of the "Private Confidant" app concept. We need to understand if students will trust it, use it, and find it genuinely helpful.

---

### **1. Introduction & Warm-up (5 mins)**

- "Hi [Name], thanks for chatting with me. My name is [Your Name], and I'm trying to better understand the experience of students like you who are preparing for these incredibly tough exams."
- "I'm not a teacher or from an institute. This is just a casual chat to hear about your experiences. There are no right or wrong answers."
- "To start, what subjects are you focusing on right now? What's your favorite (or least favorite) topic to study?"

---

### **2. Problem Discovery: The Lived Experience (15 mins)**

_Our goal is to get them to describe their study process and the associated feelings._

- "Can you walk me through what you did for your studies yesterday, from start to finish?"
  - (Probe: "How did you decide what to study? How did you feel when you started? How did you feel when you finished?")
- "Tell me about a time you felt like you were putting in a lot of hours, but your test scores weren't improving. What did that feel like?"
  - (Listen for: anxiety, guilt, confusion, "I don't know what I'm doing wrong").
- "What's the most frustrating part of managing your own study schedule day-to-day?"
- "How do you know if you've had a 'good' or 'bad' study day?"
- "What tools, apps, or methods do you use right now to plan your studies or keep track of your progress?"
  - (Probe: "What do you like about them? What do you wish they did better?")

---

### **3. Solution Exploration: Gauging the Concept (10 mins)**

_Now we introduce the app concept in a simple, relatable way._

- "I'm working on an idea for an app, and I'd love your honest feedback."
- "Imagine an app that's like a private coach. Before you start studying, you tell it how you're feeling – your energy, your stress level. Based on that, it helps you choose a realistic goal for the session."
- "It's completely private. Your teachers and parents can't see what you tell it. Its only job is to help you stay on track without adding more pressure."
- "For example, on a day you're feeling totally exhausted, it might suggest just reviewing one concept for 15 minutes instead of pushing you to do a 3-hour practice test."
- "What's your first reaction to that idea?"
- "What would be the best thing about an app like that?"
- "What would be the worst thing? What would make you want to delete it immediately?"
  - (Probe: "Would it feel like more work? Would you trust its advice? Would you worry about the privacy?")

---

### **4. Wrap-up (5 mins)**

- "This has been super helpful. Your feedback is exactly what I was looking for."
- "Is there anything else you think I should know about the student experience that we didn't cover?"
- "Thank you again for your time. Your perspective is incredibly valuable."
